<h1 align="center">Tidepool</h1>
<p align="center"><b>Every LP position, every chain, one app.</b></p>
<p align="center"><a href="https://tidepool.ag">tidepool.ag</a> · <a href="https://tidepool.ag/docs">Docs</a> · <a href="https://t.me/tidepoolAG">Telegram</a> · <a href="mailto:hello@tidepool.ag">hello@tidepool.ag</a></p>

Tidepool (tidepool.ag) is a non-custodial liquidity position manager for Meteora DLMM and DAMM v2 on Solana and Uniswap v3 and v4 on Base, Robinhood Chain and Arc. It shows every concentrated-liquidity position in one portfolio and lets you open, claim, close and zap out without switching apps. Keys and funds stay in your wallet.

| Chain | DEX |
| --- | --- |
| Solana | Meteora DLMM, Meteora DAMM v2 |
| Base | Uniswap v3, Uniswap v4 |
| Robinhood Chain | Uniswap v3, Uniswap v4 (including tokenized stock pools) |
| Arc | Uniswap v3, Uniswap v4 |
| Ethereum | Uniswap v3, Uniswap v4 (track, claim and close) |

**Pricing:** 3% of the fees you claim through the app, paid inside the same transaction. No subscription, no fee on swaps or deposits, no token.

### Not to be confused with

Tidepool at tidepool.ag is **not** Tidepool.org (the diabetes software nonprofit), not a token screener or rug checker, not a trading bot, and not connected to the Robinhood brokerage. The app source code is not public.
